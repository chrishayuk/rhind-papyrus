# Problems 61-84 (with 61B, 82B) and Numbers 85-87

Transcribed by a coding agent's sub-agent from page images of Chace vol. II
(PDF pp. 213-266) and Peet 1923 (PDF pp. 107-133, printed pp. 103-129; PDF =
printed + 4 throughout this range). No person has checked these readings.
Chace's literal translation (odd pages) was read at 300 dpi; Chace's plates
(even pages) were consulted only for layout. Peet was read for every problem.

Conventions used in this range (see the `%` header of each file):

- **Hundred-hekat notation.** In Nos. 68, 76, 82, 82B, 83, 84 and 86 large
  quadruple-hekat amounts are written with fractions of 100 (`'2 10 '4 8` =
  50 + 10 + 25 + 8 = 93). Chace prints them this way. Where such a figure sits
  in a sentence it is kept inside the quotes as Chace prints it and its value
  is given in a `%` note; it is never fed to `rhind` as a number, because the
  notation would read `'2` as one half.
- **ro.** A ro sign standing alone means one ro (Chace, note to No. 47); written
  `1 2/3 ro`, `1 ro`. Ditto marks are not copied.
- **The '6 sign without its dot** (Nos. 61, 67, 70). The scribe writes the
  special ligature for '6 without the fractional dot; Chace prints it as a
  plain `6` (No. 61 note 2), Peet as '6. Because the sign is the '6 ligature
  and not the numeral 6, the files write `'6` and say so on the line. The
  ordinary numerals written without their dot (9, 36, 24, 18, 42, 44, 39, 32)
  are copied as whole numbers, as Chace prints them.

## No. 61 (PDF 213-214; Peet 103-104)

Table of fractions of fractions, as `Multiply b by a. Result c.` pairs.
Confident: all surviving lines. Lost: about five lines between line 9 and the
'5 line; Chace restores nothing; Peet restores lines 10-14 conjecturally
(['9 ?], ['9 ?], ['5, 2/3 of it is '10 '30], ['5, '3 of it is '15],
['5, '2 of it is '10]); written `[...]` with Peet's restorations in a note.

Disagreements: Chace prints the dotless numerals as whole numbers (`'3 9`,
`'12 36`, `24`, `18 ['54]`, `'14 42`, `44`), Peet as fractions throughout.
Peet restores ['3 of it is '21]? and ['4 of it is '28]? beside the '7 lines;
Chace has nothing there. Peet restores the 2 of '42 ('4[2]); Chace has 42 whole.

Errors pointed out: Chace: fractional dot omitted in '9 (line 1), '36, '24,
'18, '42, '44; the '6 sign written without its dot; lines 5-8 were first written
in another form and altered (Chace note 3, Peet p. 103); line 9 is written again
in the margin; Peet: "2/3 <of it>" omitted in the '7 line.

## No. 61B (Chace PDF 213-214; Peet 104)

## No. 62 (PDF 215-216; Peet 104-107)

Confident. The working is three rows (12 48 / 6 24 / 3 12) and "21 Total 84".
The rows are written as a column with first numbers 12, 6, 3; there is no `1`
row and no multiplicand stated as such, so `rhind` must infer 4.
Error: "Add thou what is given for a sha'ty" — Chace: the scribe should have
written deben, not sha'ty; Peet: "ring (sic, read deben)".

## No. 63 (PDF 217-218; Peet 107)

Error: the '2 is omitted from the list to be added (Chace; Peet: <'2>).
Error: in the final list "'3 of 400 to 1: 113 '3" — Chace: "Mistake for 133";
Peet prints 133 '3 without remark (disagreement in what is printed).

## No. 68 (PDF 227-228; Peet 111-112)

Readings confident; layout simplified. Four columns of doubling; the ticked
rows are written as Chace prints them (first: 4, 8; second: 8; third: 2;
fourth: 4). Disagreements: in the third column Peet marks the 4 row "(sic 4)"

Peet restores "[+ 1 2/3 ro]" whole. In the fourth column's first row Chace says
the patch-writer omitted 1 2/3 ro (restored). Chace notes "barley" where Peet by
a slip says "corn". Errors: the hekat sign after 100 in line 1 is superfluous
and misplaced (Chace, Peet); "100 hekat" in the last column of the summary
should have no hekat sign (Chace, Peet). The 6 in the second row resembles
the abstract 6 of 2 ÷ 59 (Chace). Totals '4 15 (= 40) etc. are kept in quotes.

## No. 81 (PDF 253-254; Peet 123-124)

Least certain of the range: many small figures, ditto marks and restorations.
Chace: "There are many numerical mistakes in the original and they remain
uncorrected here" (corrections in his vol. I, pp. 113-115); Peet: "The text is
very incorrect." Rows are written hekat | hinu | part of a hekat; the third
column is red in the third part (and on the left, black, in the second part).
Disagreements: second part row 6 — Chace reads the sign as '2 hekat ("clearly"),
Peet as '4 (a "hybrid between '4 and '2"); third part '64 hekat line — Chace
says the scribe wrote ro '3 and crossed it out (so nothing), Peet prints
"'64 + 1 '3 ro (sic)" and "Read 1 2/3"; restorations: Chace restores 8 '2 ['4]
and [3] '2 '4, Peet leaves them unrestored with (sic). Errors marked by Peet
(sic): 6 '2 '16 hinu; "'5 (? 5/8)"; "3 (? 3/8)"; "(3 '4 '8) + 2/3 (sic)";
"'7 (sic)"; "'30 (? sic)"; "1 2/3 ro (sic) ... The 1 is certain, though
wrong"; "'60 (sic)"; "8 '2 (sic)"; "('2 + '4) (sic)". Chace: 30 of peculiar
form (perhaps 20 corrected); fractional dot omitted in '32 (written 32).
Peet: the B.M. Facsimile gives a stroke for the '6 ligature in '16.
Not held by the notation: three-column table rows and the ditto marks; the
fact that the third column in part B stands left of the others.

## No. 82 and 82B (PDF 255-256; Peet 124-125)

Readings confident; meaning obscure. All amounts above 10 hekat are hundred
notation and kept in quotes; one subtraction (100 − 6 '2 '8 '32 hekat 3 '3 ro
= 93 '4 '16 '64 hekat 1 2/3 ro) is stated in the papyrus as "taken away" and
"remainder" and is written as a statement. Errors: Chace: "Mistakes in lines
11, 13, and 15 are corrected in volume 1" (82: double hekat 47 '2 '4 '64 hekat
3 '3 ro; 82B: 12 '4 for ten days, and the final double-hekat figure); Peet: 93 '3
halved is 46 2/3, "wrongly given as (47 '2 '4 '64) hekat + 3 '3 ro"; 82B "12 '4
hekat (read 12 '2)"; the answer "should be 23 '3 hekat". Peet notes the unique
use of the hundred-sign with '3 for 33 '3 hekat; Chace the special sign for 8
hekat. Peet reads '6 '6 as '3 (M.K. hieratic); Chace (No. 86 note) says there
is no evidence that '6 only was meant (a disagreement of interpretation).

## No. 83 (PDF 257-258; Peet 125-126)

Confident. Disagreement: the bird names — Chace: ser-goose ('32 '64 hekat
1 ro) then set-duck ('64 hekat 3 ro), noting the scribe transposed the first
consonants of the two words; Peet: set-bird then ser-bird. Errors: the first

## No. 84 (PDF 259-260; Peet 127)

Readings follow Chace, who follows Peet "in the main". Only the sum of the
second food column (2 + 6 + 2 = 10) is written as a computation; the rest is
quoted. Errors: the 8 of the first total is the 10-hekat strokes added as units
(Chace; Peet "8 (sic)"); the month figures and the double-hekat figures are
wrong (Chace: "obviously incorrect"; Peet: three times 90 given as 200, three
times 75 as 90, half of 200 as 61 '2 '8 hekat 3 ro, half of 90 as 30). Peet
prints "9 (?)" for the spelt figure where Chace reads the special 9-hekat sign.

## Numbers 85-87 (PDF 261-266; Peet 128-131)

Quoted words only. 85: Chace gives a translation of enigmatic writing (after
Gunn); Peet declines to translate (disagreement). 86: Peet's line-by-line
values (90, 192, 146 '2 (?), 185 (?), 196, 410) are noted; Chace says Peet
interchanged "spelt" and "barley", and drops the last four repeated lines
(Peet: the last line "is repeated five times"). 87: Chace reads "commander of
the army" (mr ms'), Peet's Plate Y pn-rsy (?); Peet: a sign must have been
omitted in "caused his voice to be [heard]".

## What the notation could not hold

- Hundred-hekat notation (fractions of 100 hekat); kept in quotes with values in notes.
- Ditto marks, and the side-by-side columns of Nos. 68, 81, 82, 83, 84.
- Red ink only partly marked inside quoted sentences that mix red and black;
  where a sentence starts in red the red part is quoted separately in braces.
- Columns with no `1` row or no stated multiplicand (Nos. 62, 79, 80, 81) —
  `rhind` must infer the multiplicand.
