# Problems 24-40: notes

All reading was done by a coding agent's sub-agent from page images of the two
editions: Chace vol. II PDF pp. 141-172 (the literal translation and the plate
for each problem; rendered at 200, 400 and 600 dpi and cropped) and Peet 1923
PDF pp. 65-82 (printed pp. 61-78; PDF = printed + 4 holds throughout this
range). No person has checked these readings. No figure was taken from OCR.
Each file was run through `rhind --check`; what it still reports is listed
under each problem and is either a scribal slip the editions note or a layout
the notation cannot hold.

Files: `papyrus/no-24.rmp` to `papyrus/no-40.rmp`.

## Conventions (as in the notes for Nos. 1-23 and 41-60)

- **Omitted fractional dots.** Where Chace says the scribe left out the part
  sign, the figure is written bare (`30` for '30) with a `%` note quoting
  Chace. Peet usually prints the corrected fraction without comment; this is
  not counted as a disagreement below unless Peet comments.
- **Whole numbers written with a part sign** (Chace's "marked with the
  fractional dot in the original"): Nos. 33 (four times), 36 (twice), 38. In
  the column of No. 32 (`912`) the figure is written as a whole number with
  a note; in the red values (quoted text) it is written `'3621` etc. as it
  stands.
- **Red auxiliary values** (values of fractions in a common denominator) are
  written at the end of their row, in braces, where they belong to a column
  row (No. 33 proof); otherwise as a quoted line of red figures under the
  line they belong to (Nos. 32, 34, 36, 37, 38).
- **Red words** are braced inside the quotation: `"{A quantity, '7 of it
  added to it,} becomes it: 19."`
- **Totals the notation would misread** (a total of some rows only, of rows
  in other units, or following a column with no ticks where the scribe meant
  some rows) are quoted, with a note saying what they add.
- Chace's "Operate on N for the finding of M" is written `Reckon with N to
  find M.` only where the column does exactly that.

## What the notation could not hold

- Columns run on without a heading (No. 29's totals of a total and a row;
  No. 32's rows of the quantity with no base row; No. 33's division whose
  base is set by a row with a dot slip).
- Multipliers that are phrases ("'3 of '3 of it", "'9 of it", No. 37).
- Columns of shares with all multipliers 1 (No. 40), and side-by-side share
  columns (No. 39).
- Totals of some rows only, or of rows of mixed hekat and ro (Nos. 31, 33,
  34, 35, 38): quoted.
- Red values in another denominator (Nos. 32-34, 36-38): kept but unchecked.
- The scribe's placement of the 97 calculation at the end of No. 38 (moved
  to No. 31, as both editions do).
