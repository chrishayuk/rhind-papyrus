# Problems 1-23 (with 7B): transcription notes

Coding-agent sub-agents did all the reading, from page images of Chace vol. II
(PDF pp. 115-140, printed as photographs of the book) and Peet 1923 (PDF pp.
54-64, printed pp. 50-60). No person has checked these readings. Figures were
read from 300 dpi renders of Chace and 250 dpi renders of Peet. The OCR text
was not used for any figure.

Files: `papyrus/no-01.rmp` … `no-23.rmp`, `no-07b.rmp`.

## Conventions used throughout

- **Omitted fractional dots.** Chace often notes that the scribe left out the
  fractional dot, and prints the bare figure, e.g. `30` for 1/30. The files
  write these figures as written, as whole numbers (`2/3 '10 30`), with a `%`
  note on the line quoting Chace (e.g. `30 for '30`). `rhind` reports them as
  part-sign slips. Each case is listed below.
- **Red auxiliary numbers** (Nos. 7, 7B, 8, 13, 14, 15, 19, 20, 23). The
  papyrus writes these in red *beneath* each fraction, giving its value as so
  many parts of a common denominator. In the column problems (7-20) they are
  written at the end of the row they belong to, in braces, one per fraction,
  in the same order as the fractions. The notation's "first two numbers are
  checked, the rest kept" rule keeps them out of the arithmetic. They are not
  checked: they are in other units (28ths, 18ths) and the notation has no way
  to say so. In Nos. 21-23, where there is no column, they are kept as quoted
  text with the red in braces.
- **Red headings** are written inside the quoted sentence in braces, e.g.
  `"{The making of loaves 6 for} man [10]."`.
- **Restored ticks.** Where Chace's bracket opens before a tick (`[\2`), the
  tick is restored as well. The notation has no way to bracket a tick, so the
  file writes `\ [2]` and says so in the `%` note.
- The "Make thou the multiplication: X times 10" of Nos. 1-6 is written as
  `Multiply X by 10.`, with Chace's words in the note. The "Operate on N for
  the finding of M" of Nos. 21-22 (wꜣḥ-tp m N r gmt M) is written as `Reckon
  with N to find M.` (Peet's own translation).

## Per problem

### No. 1 (Chace pl. 34; Peet p. 51)
- Confident: \8 row `2/3 '10 30`; Total 1.
- Rows 1, 2 and 4 are restored by Chace (`[1 '10 / \2 '5 / 4 '3 '15]`),
  including the tick on 2.
- Omitted dot: `30` in the \8 row (Chace fn 6, "Fractional dot omitted").
- Peet: no `1 '10` row at all; starts at `[\2 '5`. Otherwise the same. Peet
  prints 1/30 with no comment on the dot.
- Word restorations differ slightly: Chace "Make [thou]", Peet "[You] are to
  mult[iply]".

### No. 2 (Chace pl. 34; Peet pp. 51-52)
- Confident: `2 '3 '15`, `[4] 2/3 '10 '30`, `'15` at the end of row 8.
- Restored: row 1 `[1 '5]`, the 4, row 8 `[\8 1 '3 '5]`, `[Total 2]`, the
  2 of the heading and "[man 10]", and the '5 of "'5 times 10".
- **Disagreement:** Chace has no tick on row 2; Peet restores one (`[\]2`).
  Peet also omits the `1 '5` row.
- No error noted by either. Note that only row 8 is ticked in Chace, so a
  checker summing ticked rows will get 1 '3 '5 '15, not 2. This is how Chace
  prints it.

### No. 3 (Chace pl. 35; Peet p. 52)
- Restorations agree between the two: `[1 '2] '10`, `[\2 1] '5`, `[4 2] '3
  '15`, `4 [2/3 '10] 30`; heading `[10]` and `['2]`.
- Omitted dot: `30` in the \8 row (Chace fn 4). Chace fn 3: the '5 in row 2 has
  only a faint dot on the papyrus.

### No. 4 (Chace pl. 36; Peet pp. 52-53)
- Omitted dot: `30` in the 4 row (Chace: "The scribe has omitted the
  fractional dot in the '30 in the multiplication").
- **Disagreements** (restorations; Chace places New York fragments that Peet
  left unplaced):
  - Heading "there result 7": Chace reads the 7, Peet restores it ("result
    [7]").
  - Row 1: Chace `[1] 2/3 ['30]`; Peet `[1 2/3 '30]` (2/3 also restored).
  - Row 2: Chace `\[2 1] '3 '15`, tick and '3 read on N.Y. fragments (fns 5,
    6); Peet `[\2 1'3] '15`, tick and '3 restored.
- Chace fn 2: the scribe wrote a redundant r in ḫpr(r) (a writing slip, not
  a figure).

### No. 5 (Chace pl. 37; Peet p. 53)
- Omitted dot: `30` in row 1 (Chace).
- **Disagreements:**
  - Heading: Chace reads `2/3 '10 '30`; Peet `2/3 '10 [+ '30]`.
  - Row 2: Chace `1 '2 ['10]` (trace of '2 on the N.Y. fragment, fn 2); Peet
    `1 ['2 + '10]`.
  - Row 3: Chace restores only `[3 '5]`; Peet also restores the multiplier
    (`[4 3'5]`).
- Peet notes the word mitt before line 3 "is a complete puzzle"; not in
  Chace's translation as such (Chace has "mytt" as "[The producing] of the
  same").

### No. 6 (Chace pl. 38; Peet p. 53)
- Omitted dots: `30` twice, rows 1 and 2 (Chace: "(twice)").
- **Disagreement (Peet misprint):** Peet's text has "Total 8 loaves"; Chace fn
  2: "By a misprint Peet's text has 8 here. The number is correct on his
  Plate F." File has 9.
- Peet: "the doing as it occurs" has slipped out of position (before "you are
  to multiply"). The file keeps the papyrus order.

### No. 7 (Chace pl. 39; Peet p. 54)

### No. 7B (Chace pl. 40; Peet p. 54)

- Red values only on the last row (1 '2 '4, '4). Peet: "identical with No. 7:

### No. 8 (Chace pl. 40; Peet p. 55)
- Red values in 18ths: 4 '2, 3, 1 '2, and a red 9 beneath the total.
- **Could not hold:** the red 9 under "Total '2" has no place in a `Total`
  statement and is in the `%` note only.
- **Uncertain:** Chace's general note says the scribe "omitted the fractional
  dots … in '9 in Problems 8 and 10". The only 9 in No. 8 is the red 9, which
  is a whole number of 18ths (Peet: "The three values add up to 9"). It is
  not clear which figure Chace means; the file writes 9 as a whole number.

### No. 9 (Chace pl. 40; Peet p. 55)
- **Major disagreement:** Chace reads `'2 '14 / '4 '28 / '8 '56`, Total 1
  (correct). Peet reads `'2 '10 / '4 '20 / '8 '50` and calls the sum "clearly
  incorrect". Chace fn 1: the unit figures 4, 8 and 6 are on the papyrus but
  were left out of the British Museum Facsimile, where they come at the edge
  of a plate; Peet followed the Facsimile. File follows Chace.

### No. 10 (Chace pl. 40; Peet p. 55)
- Row '4 is a bare `9` on the papyrus.
  - Chace: dot omitted, i.e. '9, and "'9 was written as a half of '7" (part
    of "a curious mistake running through Problems 10, 11, 12, and 14").
  - Peet: reads 9, "a gross error in the halving of '7, the 2 and the 7 having
    been added instead of multiplied, giving 9 instead of '14 … the error is
    clearly due to a stupid copyist."
  - File: the bare `9`, as the papyrus has it, with both readings in the note. Either way the row is
    wrong and the total '2 is right for '4 '28, '7, '14.

### No. 11 (Chace pl. 41; Peet p. 55)

  and keeps the '14.
- Row '4: `'18` (Chace: left unchanged; Peet: "'18 for '28").

### No. 12 (Chace pl. 41; Peet p. 56)

### No. 21 (Chace pl. 44; Peet pp. 58-59)
- The "10 1" beneath 2/3 '15 is written as a list one to a line, so that
  `Total 11.` can check it. On the papyrus the two figures stand side by side
  under the fractions.
- "remainder: 4" is written `Remainder 4.`. The subtraction it reports (15 −
  11, in 15ths) is not written on the papyrus, so a checker has nothing to
  check it against.
- The proof's "10 3 1 1" (under 2/3 '5 '15 '15, no total written) is kept as
  quoted text.
- **Ink disagreement:** Chace prints 10 1 and 10 3 1 1 in black (text and
  plate). Peet prints them in italics, his convention for red in these
  additions.
- Chace fn 3: "Example of proof" is "extremely doubtful" here. Chace fn 4:
  "Another, '5 '10 is: to be added" has "crept in through error". Peet: these
  words come partly from No. 35 (tp n śỉty) and perhaps No. 22.

### No. 23 (Chace pl. 46; Peet pp. 59-60)
- No column; everything is quoted text, red in braces.
- Omitted dot: `30` in the proof line (Chace fn 2).
- In the second row of values, 11 '4 is black while the rest are red. Both

## What the notation could not hold

1. The position of the red auxiliary numbers beneath their fractions. They
   are put at the end of the row, or in quoted text in Nos. 21-23. Nothing
   checks them.
2. The red 9 beneath the total in No. 8 (in a note only).
3. A restored tick (`[\2`): written `\ [2]`, with a note.
4. A restored word "Total" (No. 2): written `Total [2].`, with a note.
5. Corrections in lighter ink (Nos. 11, 12): the added '14 is kept as an
   extra number on the row. The over-written '18 of No. 12 cannot be shown.
6. "Remainder 4." in No. 21 reports a subtraction the papyrus does not write
   (15 − 11 in 15ths). `rhind --check` reports it as wrong ("the remainder is
   11; the papyrus has 4"), so that report is an artefact of the notation, not
   a scribal error.
7. The aligned layout of Nos. 21-23 (values written under fractions across
   one line).
