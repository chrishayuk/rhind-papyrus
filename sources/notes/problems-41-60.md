# Problems 41-60 (with 59B): notes

All reading was done by a coding agent's sub-agent from page images of the two
editions (Chace vol. II PDF pp. 173-212, the literal translation and the
plate for each problem; Peet 1923 PDF pp. 84-106, printed pp. 80-102). No
person has checked these readings. Figures were read from the rendered
images (150 and 300 dpi, with crops), never from OCR.

Files: `papyrus/no-41.rmp` to `papyrus/no-60.rmp` and `papyrus/no-59b.rmp`.

## General: what the notation could not hold

- **Figures.** Nos. 44, 48, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 56, 57, 58, 59/59B and 60
  have drawings (square, circle in square, rectangle, triangles, trapezoid,
  pyramids, the "pillar") with numbers written on them. These are described
  in `%` notes only.
- **"'10 of '10 of it"** rows (Nos. 44, 45, 46, 49) and "'10 of 100,000"
  (No. 49) cannot be a column multiplier, so they are kept as quoted words.
- **Columns whose rows are of a different base.** The scribe often runs one
  multiplication on into the next, or takes a fraction of a later product:
  No. 41 (`'2 320` is half of 640, and `'10 96`, `'20 48` are of 960);
  No. 42 (`'2 395 ...` is half of the `10` row; `'10`, `'20` are of 1185);
  No. 43, 44, 45, 46, 49 (several short columns run together with no
  heading between them); No. 53 (`'2 7 '2 '4 '8` is half the total, not of
  the first row). The notation has no way to start a new column except a
  statement, and the papyrus writes none, so `rhind` will check such rows
  against the wrong base. These are layout, not scribal errors.
- **Thousands-of-land (No. 48, 50, 51, 52, 53).** Areas above 10 setat are
  written as a count of thousands-of-land (each 10 setat) and setat. No. 48
  is written in setat with the papyrus's form in a note; No. 51 and 52 keep
  the papyrus's bare "2" and "20" inside quotes.
- **Cubit-strips / cubits-of-land (mh, 1/100 setat), Nos. 53-55.** There is
  no such unit. In Nos. 54 and 55 it is written as `cubit` after the setat
  part (`'2 setat 10 cubit`); `rhind` will not know that 100 cubit = 1 setat.
  In No. 53 the one occurrence is kept in quotes.
- **Palms and fingers (Nos. 56-59B).** The seked answers ("palm 5 finger 1")
  are kept in quotes; there is no palm or finger unit.
- **Special setat fraction signs** (Nos. 53-55) are written as ordinary unit
  fractions with `setat`.
- **Quadruple hekat.** In Nos. 41-47 "hekat" is the quadruple hekat; the
  notation's `hekat` is the plain hekat. Kept as written.

## Per problem

### No. 41 (Chace pl. 63; Peet pp. 80-81)
Cylindrical granary, diameter 9, height 10. Read with confidence.
- Disagreement: tick on the `'20 48` row (Chace ticks it; Peet does not).
- No errors pointed out.

### No. 42 (Chace pl. 64; Peet pp. 81-82)
Granary 10 by 10. Long fractions read carefully at 300 dpi; confident.
- Errors: 79 '108 '324 × 10 is given as 790 '18 '27 '54 (Peet: a slight
  error, should be 790 10/81; difference '81). Chace n.3: the scribe omitted
  fraction dots on several numbers (9, 6, 36, multiplier 6, 36, 10).
- The final "5900 hekat '4" is read by Chace as 5900 + 25 hekat.

### No. 43 (Chace pl. 65; Peet pp. 82-85)
Granary 9 by 6. Confident.
- Errors: dimensions transposed in the statement (Chace and Peet: 9 is the
  diameter, 6 the height). '20 of 455 '9 is written 22 '2 '4 '45; Chace n.4
  "This should be '180", Peet (sic), read '180. The words "in grain hekat"
  crept in by a copyist's error (Chace n.5; Peet: "to be omitted"). Chace
  n.2: the word "multiply" first omitted, then inserted above the line.
- The final answer 2200 hekat 50 25 '2 '32 '64 2 ro '2 '4 '36 is kept in
  quotes (mixed hundreds-of-hekat notation).

### No. 44 (Chace pl. 66; Peet pp. 84-85)
Rectangular granary 10 × 10 × 10. Confident.
- Errors: "of 10 in" surplus in the statement (Chace n.3, Peet); redundant
  "hekat" (Chace n.5); a stray fractional dot on the 10 multiplier (Chace
  n.6).
- Peet suggests the last three lines are a proof, or strayed from No. 45.

### No. 45 (Chace pl. 67; Peet p. 85)
Inverse of No. 44. Confident.
- Error: the scribe forgot the hekat-sign after 75 (Chace n.1 citing Peet);
  Chace restores [00 hekat].

### No. 46 (Chace pl. 68; Peet p. 87)
Granary of 2500 quadruple hekat: 10 × 10 × 3 '3. Confident.
- Chace n.2: a fractional dot apparently written by mistake on the 20.

### No. 47 (Chace pl. 69; Peet pp. 87-88)
Table of '10 ... '100 of 100 quadruple hekat in Horus-eye fractions and ro.
Read with fair confidence; the '70 and '90 rows (`1 '4 '8 '32 '64 hekat
2 '14 '21 '42 ro`, `1 '16 '32 '64 hekat '2 '18 ro`) are the hardest.
- Chace n.2: "when becomes it" unnecessary; Peet restores a word lost by
  haplography.
- Uncertain: a small mark Chace prints over a '2 in the '90 row, not
  explained.
- Each row is "part, amount" rather than a doubling; `rhind` will read the
  multiplier as of 100 hekat, but the column's first row "1 100" is not
  written.

### No. 48 (Chace pl. 70; Peet pp. 88-89)
No words: square of side 9 against circle of diameter 9 (8 × 8 = 64 setat,
9 × 9 = 81 setat). Confident.
- Chace n.1: the 6 is a special sign resembling 60; Peet n.3: "The text has
  60 setat". Written as setat; the papyrus's thousand-of-land form in notes.

### No. 49 (Chace pl. 71; Peet pp. 89-90)
Rectangle 10 khet by 2 khet. Confident.
- Error: the working is for 1 khet instead of 2 (Chace n.4; Peet: "If we
  read 1 khet instead of 2 ... the working will be correct").

### No. 50 (Chace pl. 72; Peet p. 90)
Circle of diameter 9 khet: 64 setat. Confident.
- Error: "60 setat 4": Chace n.2, the scribe by mistake wrote 60 or the
  special 6 of No. 48; Peet: written like 60, definitely 60.

### No. 51 (Chace pl. 73; Peet pp. 91-94)
Triangle 10 khet by 4 khet base: 20 setat. Confident.
- Disagreement (order): Peet places "You are to take half of 4 ... This is
  its acreage" before the working columns; Chace after.
- Answer "2" (thousands-of-land); Chace translates "20 setat".

### No. 52 (Chace pl. 74; Peet pp. 94-95)
Trapezoid 20 khet, bases 6 and 4. Confident.
- Errors: n.t repeated (Chace n.2). Result written 20 where 10 (thousands
  of land = 100 setat) is meant: Chace n.5 "by mistake he wrote 20 instead
  of 10"; Peet "result 10 (sic)" in the text and "20 (read 10)" at the end.

### No. 53 (Chace pl. 75; Peet pp. 95-96)
Triangle divided by two lines. No statement. Chace refers to vol. I p. 94
for its "numerical mistakes"; Peet: "clearly incomplete and incorrect".
- Disagreements: tick on the `'4 1 '8 setat` row (Peet ticks, Chace does
  not); tick on the last row `'2 7 '2 '4 '8 setat` (Chace ticks, Peet does
  not).
- Errors: multiplier '2 written as '2 setat (Chace n.1, twice; Peet). The
  total "setat 1 5 '2 '4": setat sign over the 1 instead of the 5 (Chace
  n.4); Peet reads 1 thousand-of-land and 5 '2 '4 setat. The "cubit-strip
  10" is a sign like 30 (Chace n.2; Peet n.2: means 10 cubits-of-land).
  Peet: the first calculation is right "despite inaccurate ticking and the
  introduction of an unnecessary step"; the '10 lines are "totally
  unconnected".
- "Total" in red in both totals.

### No. 54 (Chace pl. 76; Peet p. 96)
7 setat divided into 10 fields. Confident.
- Error: "setat 7" omitted by the scribe (Chace n.1 restores it; Peet too).
  Row 4: Chace n.2 "There should be a '4 here also"; Peet restores [ '4 ].
  10 cubit-strips written like 30 (Chace n.3).

### No. 55 (Chace pl. 77; Peet pp. 96-97)
3 setat divided into 5 fields. Confident.
- Error: "setat 1" for the number 5 (Chace n.1; Peet). Peet marks "5 setat
  (sic)" and puts (sic) on the multipliers '2 and '10 of the division
  column; Chace has no remark on those multipliers (counted as a
  disagreement in annotation, not in figures).

### No. 56 (Chace pl. 78; Peet pp. 97-98)
Pyramid 360 by 250; seked 5 '25 palms. Confident.
- Chace n.4: fractional dot omitted on the multiplier 5.
- The division of 180 by 250 is not written.

### No. 57 (Chace pl. 79; Peet p. 99)
Pyramid 140, seked 5 palms 1 finger; height 93 '3. No columns. Confident.
- Chace n.2: pw first omitted then added above the line.

### No. 58 (Chace pl. 80; Peet p. 99)
Pyramid height 93 '3, side 140; seked 5 palms 1 finger. Confident.
- Error/disagreement: the column row `'4 1 '4` (should be 1 '2 '4): Peet
  "1 '4 (read 1 '2 '4)"; Chace prints 1 '4 with no note. The text above has
  1 '2 '4. Chace restores [p]w.

### No. 59 (Chace pl. 81; Peet p. 100)
Pyramid, height and side transposed. Confident.
- Errors: dimensions transposed (Chace n.1; Peet "(sic)" on both). The
  answer "palm 5 finger 2": Chace n.2 "Mistake for 1".
- Disagreement: Peet translates "5 palms 1 finger" with no remark (silently
  corrected, or read differently).
- The closing words "my n m" are unexplained (Chace n.3; Peet "What .....?").

### No. 59B (Chace pl. 81; Peet p. 100)
Inverse of No. 59. Run on by the scribe as part of No. 59 (Peet).
- Errors: "a pyramid of 12" (Peet (sic); Chace n.1: ukha-thebet omitted,
  perhaps intentionally). 2/3 of 12 is given as 4: Chace n.6 "Mistake for
  8"; Peet "4 (read 8)". Chace restores [palm] and [y].
- Peet translates "7 spans" where Chace has "palm 7".

### No. 60 (Chace pl. 82; Peet pp. 100-102)
"Pillar (?)" (Chace) / "cone (?)" (Peet), base 15 cubits, height 30;
seked 4. Confident.
- Errors: "(times 4)" in the statement of the operation: Chace n.5 "The
  scribe has by mistake inserted the result in the statement"; Peet "four
  times (sic)". Peet: the scribe divides 30 by 7 '2 instead of 7 '2 by 30
  ('4 cubit = 1 3/4 palms); "a serious confusion has taken place".

## All Chace/Peet disagreements found
1. No. 41: tick on `'20 48` (Chace yes, Peet no).
2. No. 51: order of the sentence "take half of 4 ... acreage" relative to
   the working.
3. No. 53: tick on `'4 1 '8 setat` (Peet yes, Chace no); tick on
   `'2 7 '2 '4 '8 setat` (Chace yes, Peet no); Peet reads the "1" of the
   second total as a thousand-of-land, Chace as a misplaced setat sign.
4. No. 55: Peet's (sic) on the multipliers '2 and '10 (no remark in Chace).
5. No. 58: '4 of 7 written 1 '4; Peet flags it, Chace does not.
6. No. 59: "finger 2" (Chace, flagged as error) vs "1 finger" (Peet).
7. No. 59B: "7 spans" (Peet) vs "palm 7" (Chace) (translation only).
8. No. 60: "pillar" vs "cone" (translation only).
