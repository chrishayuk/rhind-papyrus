# 6. A quantity and its part

Problems 24 to 34 ask for a quantity that, with a part of itself, makes a
number: "a quantity whose seventh part is added to it becomes 19". The
scribe does not solve an equation. He guesses a number that makes the
fraction easy, sees what it gives, and scales: the method of false position.

```rmp
No. 24
"A quantity whose seventh part is added to it becomes 19."
"Reckon with 7."
  1   7
 '7   1
Total 8.
Reckon with 8 to find 19.
Result 2 '4 '8.
Multiply 2 '4 '8 by 7.
Result 16 '2 '8.
Proof.
  1   16 '2 '8
 '7   2 '4 '8
Total 19.
```

Guess 7: with its seventh it makes 8. 19 is 8 times 2 4̅ 8̅, so the quantity
is 7 times that, 16 2̅ 8̅. The proof adds the quantity and its seventh and
gets 19. rhind wrote in the working the page left out, and checked every
figure the page gives.
